# Practical visit plans checked on 6 October 2026

These notes support the 12 entries in `src/domains/drama/data/visit-plans.json`. The guides add a usable local sequence, arrival decisions, cost categories, access limits and a bad-weather decision to existing scene descriptions. They do not claim firsthand visits or guarantee AdSense approval.

## Evidence and estimates

- `checkedAt` is the research date. It is not a field inspection, phone confirmation or guarantee that a venue is open today.
- Schedule blocks are editorial estimates. They deliberately include meals, waiting, navigation and pauses. They are not measured walking times, booked departures or live taxi quotations.
- Travel to the first stop is separate from the catalog's local tour duration. Arrival text avoids promising an unverified intercity timetable.
- No complete step-free route was surveyed. The guides identify physical constraints and tell readers when the exact accessible approach needs confirmation.
- Historical prices, restaurant names and transit directions were not promoted to current operating facts. Numeric admission appears only for the palace listings and the Bank of Korea's own museum guide. The Pohang tourist-taxi price comes from the city's current public booking page, with a confirmation step because that page retains an old app notice.
- Public sources can disagree or retain old notices. Where the operator page was unavailable or its current offer unclear, the guide explains the uncertainty instead of filling in a price.
